# Integrate mean above p85 into the backtest

Date: 2026-09-03
Branch: master
Status: Complete

## Objective

Add a simulation-derived `mean_above_p85` field to the leakage-safe player
backtest. Test whether the conditional simulated mean above p85 explains more
of the higher observed scores for RB, TE, and WR. Defer QB interpretation.

## Relevant Field-Guide Entries

- `field-guide/backtesting.md` - defines p85 bins and walk-forward calibration
- `field-guide/testing.md` - requires output-shape checks and direct metric checks
- `field-guide/architecture.md` - separates player simulation from downstream team work

## Decisions

`mean_above_p85` is the mean of simulated scores strictly greater than the
player's simulated p85. `p85_tail_excess` is `mean_above_p85 - p85`. Both use
the current walk-forward outcome pool, so they do not use target-season scores.

The backtest reports two separate questions:

1. Is p85 statistically calibrated?
2. Does the upper-tail descriptor explain the size of high observed scores?

The second question uses only rows where the observed score exceeded that
row's p85. A walk-forward regression compares `p85` alone, `mean_above_p85`
alone, `p85 +` a prior-season average tail excess, the raw
`mean_above_p85`, and a fitted `p85 + p85_tail_excess` model.

## Results

Across RB, TE, and WR, the new feature is a good description of the conditional
upper-tail mean. The observed tail mean minus predicted tail mean is +0.137 RB,
+0.178 TE, and +0.292 WR fantasy points when bins are weighted by actual tail
rows.

The feature gives a small improvement for individual tail rows when used as its
raw conditional mean. Across 979 walk-forward exceedances from 2024 and 2025:

| Method | RMSE | MAE | Correlation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| p85 only, linear calibration | 5.128 | 3.791 | 0.689 |
| p85 plus prior tail excess | 5.108 | 3.806 | 0.689 |
| raw mean above p85 | 5.080 | 3.769 | 0.687 |
| p85 plus fitted tail excess | 5.132 | 3.791 | 0.689 |

The raw `mean_above_p85` improves RMSE by 0.93 percent and MAE by 0.58
percent against the p85-only linear baseline. RMSE improves for each position:
1.69 percent for RB, 0.52 percent for TE, and 0.64 percent for WR. The TE MAE
increases slightly, and the correlation does not improve.

The new field is highly correlated with p85, at 0.996 for RB, 0.995 for TE,
and 0.994 for WR. Tail excess varies by only about 0.6 points for RB and WR,
and 0.9 points for TE. This explains the small incremental gain.

## Final Outcome

The backtest now carries a valid simulation-based upper-tail mean. It explains
the expected size of scores above p85 at grouped position-season-p85 levels.
Its raw value gives a modest RMSE improvement for individual high-score rows,
while p85 still gives slightly stronger ordering. Use `mean_above_p85` as a
tail-size field alongside p85. Retain p85 as the calibrated quantile.

## Field-Guide Review

Proposed

### Durable Lessons

- Keep conditional tail means separate from quantile calibration.
- Evaluate p85 pointwise coverage and upper-tail severity with different metrics.
- A tail mean that is almost an affine transform of p85 may describe the tail
  well without adding independent predictive signal.

### Potential Enforcement

Keep tests that require finite `mean_above_p85` and assert that any future tail
calibration uses only rows with observed scores above the row-level p85.
